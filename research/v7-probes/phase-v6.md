## Findings

- Stall rule: the any-node version (gap in creation order) cuts many keeps at m <= 8 (53 at m=5, 23 at m=8) and loses final-best keeps in 3 to 5 runs. The lineage version (non-keep recorded ancestors before a keep) is much safer: m=8 cuts the final best in 4 runs (fold_advances_eval, interleaved_affine_gaps, putnam_1964_b2, putnam_1995_a3); m=12 cuts none of the final bests and one non-final keep (b2 node 112, lineage gap 18).
- Recommendation: m=12, scoped to the lineage (subtree of the last keep), not to all nodes. m=16 gives the same cut set on this data. The margin is one step (m=8 loses finals), and the sample is 19 runs, nearly all deepseek, so treat m=12 as a starting value to test, not a fixed constant.
- a4: last keep at 29.8% of spend. The remaining 70% (about $0.32) gave no gain over 412 nodes, and the any-node rule would fire 47 times after 38%. A stall rule would have saved that spend without changing the best. The recorded-parent depth of the final best is 11, not 28. The 28 in the brief matches the node index (ordinal 28 among all nodes). The "#19" in the run notes is probably a candidate-only count.
- b2: final best (98.54, recorded depth 46) came at 77.9% of spend. Between 52.6% and 70.5% of spend there was a 43-node stretch that gained 0.06 points. The final keep sits 8 non-keep ancestors below its last keep ancestor, which is why m=8 would have cut it.
- Phase shares: among the 10 timestamped runs, the median proxy at 30%, 75% and 90% of spend is 88%, 98% and 100% of the final. Gains flatten after about 75%, so a 10% reserve is reasonable. The 30% explore share is not supported as a fixed boundary: in two runs (WickAlgebra, interleaved_affine_gaps) the first scored node arrived at about 31-32% of spend, so the explore phase produced no proxy. Trigger phase changes on stall events and first-score events rather than fixed percentages.

## Caveats

- Costs come from turn_end usage.cost.total. Node spend is the spend at the most recent arena tool start before the ledger row, so it is approximate to within one turn.
- Nine pilot runs have no tool timestamps. Their spend is linear in node index (labelled "node"), so their budget-timing numbers are approximate. They are excluded from the pooled $ and histogram figures.
- Lineage uses parents[0] only. Merge nodes with two parents are treated by their first parent.
- Keep order is creation order, while each score may arrive later (score time is used for budget timing). The proxy is full-mode local_proxy when available, else fast estimated_proxy (which assumes full compat).
- Cut-off counts come from ledger replay, not a counterfactual run. The rule moves focus, so a cut-off keep may still be reached later from another branch.
- The lineage gap counts non-keep ancestors only. It is a lower bound on a true subtree stall, so the any-node and lineage results bracket the rule.
- "Last keep" always equals the final best by construction, so the two budget rows in section 4 are identical.
